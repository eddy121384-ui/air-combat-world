# Skyfront: Homeland — Lore & Narrative Index

Status: Working foundation / review required
Updated: 2026-10-12 (Asia/Taipei)
Scope: Narrative and worldbuilding only. No gameplay, geometry, rendering, or runtime changes.

## Purpose

Keep a compact, internally consistent reference for the fictional world behind a mobile-first, real-world-city arcade air-combat game. This is **not** a mandate to create a long campaign or exposition-heavy game.

- [World Bible v0.1](world-bible-v0.1.md) — polity, AI governance, causes of war, factions, established constraints and unanswered questions.
- [Prologue: First Contact](prologue-first-contact.md) — the Taiwan rookie pilot tutorial and its planned incident sequence.
- [Narrative Design](narrative-design.md) — radio-driven characterization, flexible player viewpoints, modes, and storytelling budgets.
- Existing cross-reference: [Skyfront Music Direction](../game-design/music-direction.md) — place, institution, and pilot as emotional layers.

## Decision vocabulary

- **CONFIRMED / 已確認**: explicitly stated by the project owner during earlier discussions. Record faithfully; changes require another explicit owner decision.
- **WORKING / 暫定**: plausible design directions or actively explored concepts; not yet binding.
- **OPEN / 未決定**: must not be filled in silently by an agent.

If two notes conflict, the most recent explicit decision of the project owner takes precedence. In particular, the **2026-10-12 correction of the war's origin** overrides descriptions in which the Commune deliberately initiates a global kinetic war for conquest.

## Guardrails for contributors and agents

- Do not conflate a zero-day cyber-coercion attack with an initial declaration of conventional global war.
- Do not describe the Commune as inherently evil or the Philosopher King as a generic rogue AI; its governance ideology and errors are central.
- Do not treat China as the default foe on the Taipei map. The prologue starts with a tense ROCAF/PLAAF intercept **without turning it into an air-to-air kill mission**.
- Do not canonize the identity or death of the rookie, mentor, AWACS, enemy ace, or named city occupations without a decision.
- Do not force all city missions, aircraft, and playable factions into one chronological pilot biography.
- Keep the initial product **offline-first, single-player, mobile-first**; a live-service backend and PvP are not launch requirements.
- Do not modify Unreal / geometry / terrain / materials as part of lore documentation.

## Change process

Future lore additions should cite the design decision or be marked WORKING. Review through a Draft PR before any canon lock. This v0.1 collects conversations; it is not final approved canon.
