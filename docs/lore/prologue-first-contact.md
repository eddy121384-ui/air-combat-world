# Skyfront: Homeland — Prologue: First Contact

Status: Working story treatment, **not** an approved cutscene script
Updated: 2026-10-12 (Asia/Taipei)
Prologue location: Northern Taiwan and adjacent offshore airspace
Format: In-play tutorial + escalating incident; target short and replayable on a phone.

## 1. Player-established story beats — CONFIRMED

1. The player begins as a **rookie pilot of the Republic of China Air Force** on an apparently routine sortie, with flight-control instruction woven into the flight.
2. An emergency call reports **PLAAF aircraft approaching northern Taiwan's offshore airspace**. The player and experienced lead pilot are redirected to intercept / drive off the approaching aircraft.
3. Player and Chinese fighters maneuver in a tense confrontation. **It is a standoff, not a scripted shooting war between Taiwan and China.**
4. Communications / data links suddenly fail. At first the incident appears plausibly attributable to the opposing Chinese aircraft.
5. The Chinese aircraft abruptly disengage and depart. A distant major explosion is visible at sea.
6. An unidentified drone formation arrives. The current encounter is replaced by an emergency combat assignment to stop the unknown attackers.
7. **The lead pilot is shot down while protecting the rookie.** The emotional hinge is losing the pilot who has just taught and accompanied the player.
8. The tutorial mission ends; only then does the opening/title/global-world introduction reveal that the incident belongs to a much broader crisis.

The status of the downed pilot (**confirmed killed vs ejected/missing**) remains OPEN. Do not casually turn “shot down” into “killed.”

A production aircraft candidate is the **F-16V**, already part of the Taipei vertical-slice plan; an exact story loadout is WORKING.

## 2. Gameplay/drama integration — WORKING

The following is a proposed mission-beat structure, not locked pacing.

### Beat A — Ordinary sky

- Teach yaw/roll/pitch or the final mobile-control equivalents, throttle/turning, target camera, and basic cues via a natural wingman conversation.
- Anchor the player in the northern Taiwan environment with coast, mountains or recognizable skyline when technically feasible.
- Give the lead pilot a memorable mannerism or repeated phrase, but do not write their entire biography.

### Beat B — Interception without fire

- Transition from routine tutorial to tense off-coast approach and tracking of PLAAF aircraft.
- Teach relative positioning, situational awareness and nonlethal target observation.
- Do not instruct the player to shoot down PLAAF aircraft to graduate from the tutorial.

### Beat C — Something is wrong

- Disrupt information flow rather than instantly muting every audio channel without explanation.
- Show the opposing fighters break off unexpectedly, then a distant explosion and new radar anomalies.
- Allow the player to perceive the reversal before any omniscient exposition.

### Beat D — First live engagement

- Reveal hostile drones, teach locking, firing and evasion within a small understandable combat encounter.
- Provide a believable trigger for weapons authorization and usable aircraft stores (exact air-defense posture remains OPEN).
- Make the mentor's protective action understandable in flight terms, **not merely a forced death animation unrelated to the player's situation**.
- If the lead is lost mid-combat, keep the final new-player fight survivable and retry-friendly.

### Beat E — Consequences and title

- After the player survives, let local radio traffic and/or short broadcasts reveal similarly unexplained emergencies elsewhere.
- Show SKYFRONT: HOMELAND and return to playable content promptly.
- A reveal of the full Commune ideology, cyberwar history or Philosopher King's motives is **not needed during the tutorial**.

## 3. Continuity questions — OPEN

- Exact moment of this incident relative to the U.S. designation/strike and the Commune's declaration of global war.
- Who physically launched the drones, their staging location and why this offshore sector matters.
- Identity and intended target of the explosion at sea.
- How training sortie, readiness configuration and live weapons are made coherent without delaying onboarding.
- What minimum radio channels remain available after the blackout.
- Lead's identity, disposition and later role (memorial, rescue, disappearance).
- Who is the AWACS/controller on duty and which voice(s) recur in other cities.
- Exact training length, optional skip, accessibility and control-hint requirements.

## 4. Acceptance tests

The opening should work even if a player ignores geopolitical detail. It should:
- Teach a fun flight-and-combat loop before asking for lore investment.
- Make the ROCAF/PLAAF encounter tense but distinct from the unknown global attacker.
- Communicate a threat reversal via aircraft behavior, radio and visual evidence.
- Make the lead's loss understandable rather than emotionally manipulative.
- End with a reason to fly another mission; not with a mandatory exposition dump.

The player's identity after this prologue need not remain fixed to the ROC Air Force rookie.
